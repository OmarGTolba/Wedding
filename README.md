# Amr & Fatma — Wedding Invitation

A single-page wedding invitation: an envelope that opens into the site, then a
one-screen-per-step scroll through the story, the countdown, the venues, the
schedule and the details.

Everything lives in `index.html` — no build step, no dependencies. Edit the
`CONFIG` object at the top of the `<script>` to change names, date, venues and
the music file.

Photos are the `*.jpeg` files next to it, referenced by `data-src`.
